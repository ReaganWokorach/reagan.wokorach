Who i am

About Me

Wokorach Reagan is a cybersecurity professional in training and IT specialist based in Gulu, Uganda. He has completed the ALX Africa Professional Foundations programme and is now advancing through ALX Africa's Cybersecurity track with full commitment and a clear sense of purpose.

With over five years of hands-on experience in data annotation and AI training at Samasource Uganda, and a Diploma in Information Technology from Cavendish University Uganda, Reagan brings a rare combination of technical precision, analytical problem-solving, and the kind of adaptability that only comes from working in high-volume, accuracy-critical environments.

Reagan's driving mission is rooted in the Global Challenge of Good Health and Well-Being. Having grown up in northern Uganda and witnessed how fragile digital infrastructure puts communities at risk, he is building a career in cybersecurity specifically to protect the healthcare systems, government platforms, and community organisations that people across East Africa depend on.

Africa has fewer than 20,000 certified cybersecurity professionals for over a billion people. Reagan intends to be one of the professionals who closes that gap — with local context, genuine skill, and a long-term commitment to the continent.

Currently advancing toward CompTIA Security+ and the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity credential, Reagan has practical experience with Nmap, Wireshark, and Burp Suite, and brings strong skills in web development and version control.

Interesting fact: Reagan once taught himself Wireshark by capturing and analysing his own home network traffic — and discovered his neighbour's device was sending data packets at 2am. He still does not know what they were up to. But it confirmed he had chosen the right field.
